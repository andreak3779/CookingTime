Calculating Cooking Time

To calculate the time required for cooking a specific meal follow theses steps.

  1. Select the kind of meal you will be cooking from the list below "Your Cooking a:".
  2. Enter the weight in pounds or in kilograms in the text box beside "Cooking Weight:".
  3. Choose how you measured the meal in either pounds (default) or kilograms.
  4. Click "Calculate"
  5. The cooking time will be displayed in the box beside the "Cooking Time:"

If you made a mistake and need to re-enter anything just click on "Reset" which will clear all the information

and cooking time will be ready for another meal.
